Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Chase Layers ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play Token Chain.

Information we process

  • Player identity: the display name you choose, an anonymous Unity player identifier, and a locally generated installation identifier.
  • Online session information: lobby and room identifiers, selected character and map, match state, scores, connection data, and other information required to create or join multiplayer matches.
  • Local game data: settings, selected character, unlocked characters, currency, level, match statistics, and progress stored on your device.
  • Technical data: limited device, network, diagnostic, and service data that Unity Gaming Services may process to authenticate players and operate Lobby and Relay connectivity.

How we use information

We use this information to operate online multiplayer, identify players inside a match, maintain local progress and preferences, prevent technical abuse, troubleshoot problems, and keep the game reliable.

Third-party services

Token Chain uses Unity Gaming Services, including Authentication, Lobby, Relay, and Netcode. Unity may process technical and service data under its own privacy terms. Review the Unity Privacy Policy.

Advertising, purchases, and sensitive permissions

The current version does not include third-party advertising or in-app purchases. We do not sell personal information. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. A visual voice-chat setting may appear in development interfaces, but voice chat is not implemented and the microphone is not accessed.

Sharing

Your chosen display name, character, match actions, and scores can be visible to other players in the same online session. Service data may be processed by Unity only as needed to provide the online features or comply with law.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset the game, clear its storage, or uninstall it. Online service data is retained according to Unity's service rules and operational needs. You may request help with deletion by contacting us and providing the player name or anonymous player identifier shown in the game where available.

Children

Token Chain is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical measures and established service providers to protect game data. Online information may be processed in countries other than your own where Unity or its providers operate.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.
